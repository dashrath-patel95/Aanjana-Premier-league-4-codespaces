About APL

Anjana Premier League started as an idea among the youth of Sundha Patti's villages — a way to bring everyone onto one ground for something other than a wedding or a festival. Four editions in, it's grown into the thing people plan their November around.

It's still, at heart, community cricket. Players who work in fields and shops all year get a few days to open their shoulders and bowl fast. Villages that mostly know each other through family ties get to needle one another over a run-out instead. The Samaj organizes it, but the tournament belongs to whoever picks up a bat.

What APL asks of everyone who steps onto the Matraji Pal ground is simple: play hard, play fair, and shake hands at the end regardless of the scoreboard.

  • 🏏

    Cricket first

    A straight knockout format built around real overs on a real ground, not exhibition matches.

  • 👥

    Youth-led

    Organized and volunteered largely by the younger generation of Sundha Patti.

  • 🏷️

    Built on tradition

    Rooted in the Anjana Kalbi Chaudhary Samaj's history of bringing villages together.

  • 🤝

    Sportsmanship over scoreline

    Competitive cricket, judged as much by conduct on the field as by the result.
